      FAQ

      Questions, answered.

      I don’t know my level. Where do I start?

      For conversation lessons, the 30-minute level assessment comes first, and it sets where you start: a spoken conversation, with feedback right after and a written message with your corrected errors. For the academic course, A1 needs no test. For A2 and above, a grammar test is required and sets your level. We arrange it when you register.

      Can I join conversation as a complete beginner?

      Conversation courses start at level A2, because you need some German to speak in the lessons. If you are new to German, start with the Academic course at A1, then move to conversation.

      What is the difference between the three courses?

      Conversation (from A2) gets you speaking and thinking in German. Academic teaches the language level by level, A1 to C1, including grammar, privately or in a small group. Exams prepares you for the speaking part of Goethe or telc. Many learners combine them, and the assessment helps you choose.

      Can I learn in a group?

      Small groups are available for the academic courses (A1 to C1). Groups meet twice a week for 2 hours, usually Monday & Wednesday or Saturday & Wednesday, Cairo time. A group starts once it has enough learners. Conversation and exam preparation are private, one-to-one lessons for now.

      Do you prepare students for Goethe and telc exams?

      Yes, for the speaking part only. I do not teach the listening, reading or writing parts. SR Deutsch is independent: not affiliated with the Goethe-Institut or telc, and not an official examiner.

      Are lessons online or in person?

      Online worldwide. On-site sessions are available across Beheira, Egypt.
