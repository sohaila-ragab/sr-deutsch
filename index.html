    Conversation corner

    Conversational German, in detail.

    For learners who understand more than they can say, or who want to start speaking without fear. You do most of the talking. Topics come from your own life.

    1

    Who it is for

    Beginners to advanced learners (A1–C1) who want to speak, live, work or study in German-speaking settings, or simply feel at ease with the language.

    2

    What you work on

    Vocabulary range, grammar in speech, sentence building, flow, pronunciation and the habit of thinking in German instead of translating.

    3

    What changes

    You answer faster, recover when you get stuck, ask for clarification in German and handle everyday and work situations with confidence.

    A typical session

    How a lesson runs

    1. Warm-up
      Talk about your weekEasy start in German, at your level.
    2. Topic
      A real situationA flat viewing, a job interview, a doctor’s visit, a chat with colleagues.
    3. Fix
      Live correctionGrammar, word choice and pronunciation: sounds, rhythm and intonation.
    4. Recap
      Take-away listThe new words and structures you actually used.

    Academic corner

    Exam preparation, in detail.

    For learners with a Goethe or telc exam ahead. We plan backwards from your exam date and spend lesson time where it raises your result.

    1

    Who it is for

    Learners preparing for Goethe or telc exams for study, work, residence or personal goals, at the level they are aiming for.

    2

    What you work on

    Speaking tasks in exam format, plus the vocabulary, grammar and strategy that exam tasks reward, with timed practice.

    3

    What you get

    A clear plan, mock tests with detailed feedback and a realistic view of where you stand before exam day.

    The four skills

    Where the focus goes

    SprechenSpeaking: our strongest focus, rehearsed in exam format.
    HörenListening: strategy and timed practice.
    LesenReading: task types and time management.
    SchreibenWriting: structure and the vocabulary exams reward.
    Independent preparation. SR Deutsch is not affiliated with the Goethe-Institut or telc and is not an official examiner. Your exam is taken at an official exam centre.
    The plan

    Three phases to exam day

    1. Phase 1
      DiagnoseAn assessment shows your level and weakest areas. We set priorities and a timeline.
    2. Phase 2
      TrainSkill-by-skill practice, with exam-style speaking prompts every week.
    3. Phase 3
      Mock & polishTimed mock tests with feedback, then a final push on the details.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    I don’t know my level. Where do I start?

    Book an assessment: a 30-minute spoken conversation. You get feedback right after, plus a written message with your corrected errors and where to start.

    Do you prepare students for Goethe and telc exams?

    Yes, in the academic path, with speaking practice at the center. SR Deutsch is independent: not affiliated with the Goethe-Institut or telc, and not an official examiner.

    Which is better, private or group?

    Private is faster and fully tailored to you, with a flexible schedule. A group costs less per lesson, meets at a fixed weekly time and gives you people to speak with.

    Are lessons online or in person?

    Online worldwide. On-site sessions are available across Beheira, Egypt.
